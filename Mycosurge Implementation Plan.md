# **Mycosurge: Early Game Implementation Plan (Text-Based Prototype)**

## **1\. Core Concept & Tone**

The game is a text-driven incremental/idle experience. The UI is minimalist, relying on buttons, progressing resource counters, and a central scrolling "Event Log" that delivers the atmospheric, biological horror narrative of a growing fungal consciousness.

## **2\. Resource Architecture**

- **Water (W):** Gathered manually at first. Used for cell expansion.
- **Nutrients (N):** Gathered manually at first. Used for structural growth.
- **Biomass (B):** Synthesized from Water and Nutrients. The primary currency for upgrades and exploration.
- **Mycelial Network (MN):** Represents territory in mm. Generated by exploring. Increases idle resource caps and unlocks encounters.

## **3\. Game Phases & Mechanics (The Paradigm Shift Loop)**

### **Phase 1: The Worker (Awakening)**

- **UI State:** Blank screen, except for an event log and one button: \[Absorb Resources\].
- **Action:** Clicking generates 1 Water and 1 Nutrient.
- **Event Log:** "The substrate is dry." \-\> "A drop of dew permeates the cell wall." \-\> "Consciousness sparks." \-\> "Absorbed moisture and trace minerals."
- **Unlock:** At 10 Water and 10 Nutrients, \[Synthesize Biomass\] button appears.

### **Phase 2: The Manager (Automation)**

- **Mechanic Shift:** Manual gathering becomes tedious. The player transitions to managing an economy.
- **New Action:** \[Synthesize Biomass\] (Cost: 10W, 10N) \-\> Generates 1 Biomass.
- **Upgrades Unlocked (purchased with Biomass):**
  - _Osmotic Pump (Cost: 2B):_ \+1 Water/sec.
  - _Enzymatic Exudates (Cost: 2B):_ \+1 Nutrients/sec.
- **Event Log Integration:** Buying upgrades starts an idle loop. "Enzyme automation initiated."

### **Phase 3: The Explorer (Hyphal Expansion)**

- **Mechanic Shift:** With basic resources automated, the player needs a new strategic goal.
- **New Action:** \[Extend Hyphae\] (Cost: 5 Biomass) (Unlocked when idle generation begins).
- **Effect:** Increases "Mycelial Network" by \+1mm.
- **Event Log Integration:** "Hyphal network extended into the dark substrate."

### **Phase 4: The Tactician (Micro-Encounter)**

- **Trigger:** When Mycelial Network reaches 5mm.
- **The Paradigm Shift:** The idle resource loop pauses. The standard action buttons are cleared or disabled.
- **Event Log:** A prominent warning appears. "WARNING: Vibrations detected. A hostile, mobile organism is grazing on the outer hyphae. Nematode detected. Prepare for defense."
- **New Action:** A single button appears: \[Engage Nematode\].
- **Goal:** This forces the player to shift from passive management to active problem-solving using the resources they have accumulated.
